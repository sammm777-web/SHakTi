INDIAN CONSTITUTION

Know Your Constitutional Rights

The Constitution of India guarantees equality, dignity, freedom and protection under law. Understand the important provisions and their relevance to women's empowerment.

IMPORTANT CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS

Your Rights, Your Power

Click each card to understand the constitutional provision.

Article 14

Right to Equality

Equality before the law and equal protection of laws.

Every person is entitled to equality before the law. Women are equally protected by the legal system.

Article 15

Prohibition of Discrimination

Prohibits discrimination on grounds including sex.

The State cannot discriminate against citizens on grounds including sex. Article 15(3) also permits special provisions for women and children.

Article 16

Equal Opportunity

Equality of opportunity in public employment.

Citizens are entitled to equality of opportunity in matters relating to public employment.

Article 21

Right to Life and Liberty

Protects life and personal liberty.

No person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law. Judicial interpretation connects this protection with dignity and personal autonomy.

Article 23

Protection Against Exploitation

Prohibits human trafficking and forced labour.

Human trafficking, begar and other similar forms of forced labour are prohibited.

Article 39(d)

Equal Pay

Directive Principle concerning equal pay for equal work.

The State should direct its policy towards securing equal pay for equal work for both men and women.

Article 42

Maternity Relief

Promotes humane working conditions and maternity relief.

The State shall make provision for securing just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief.

Article 51A(e)

Fundamental Duty

Promotes dignity of women.

It is the duty of every citizen to renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women.
