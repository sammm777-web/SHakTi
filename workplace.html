WORKPLACE AWARENESS

Know Your Workplace Rights

Understanding the law helps create safe, inclusive and respectful working environments.

⚖

Equal Opportunity

Women are entitled to equality of opportunity in public employment under Article 16 of the Constitution.

🛡

Protection from Harassment

The POSH Act provides a framework for preventing and addressing sexual harassment at the workplace.

🤝

Respectful Environment

A workplace should promote dignity, professional respect and safety.

📋

Complaint Mechanism

Employees can use the applicable workplace complaint mechanism established under law.

👩‍💼

Internal Committee

Workplaces covered by the POSH Act with ten or more employees must constitute an Internal Committee.

🌸

Maternity Protection

The Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, as amended, provides maternity-related protections to eligible women employees.

Understanding the POSH Act, 2013

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 aims to protect women against sexual harassment at the workplace.


Important Features

  1. Prevention: Promote awareness and prevent workplace sexual harassment.
  2. Internal Committee: Covered workplaces with ten or more employees must establish an Internal Committee.
  3. Local Committee: District-level Local Committees provide a complaint mechanism for specified situations, including workplaces with fewer than ten workers or complaints against the employer.
  4. Complaint process: The Act provides procedures and time limits for making and addressing complaints.
  5. Confidentiality: The law protects the confidentiality of complaint-related information.

Where Can You Seek Help?

Approach your workplace Internal Committee or the appropriate Local Committee. You may also seek assistance from a qualified lawyer or legal services authority.

Remember: You deserve dignity and a safe working environment.
