STAY SAFE IN THE DIGITAL WORLD

Digital Safety & Awareness

Technology gives us opportunities, but online harassment and cybercrime can affect personal safety. Learn how to protect yourself online.

KNOW THE RISKS

Common Online Threats

Understand the different forms of cyber harassment.

💬

Cyberbullying

Repeated online intimidation, abusive messages, threats or humiliation through digital platforms.

👁

Cyberstalking

Repeated unwanted online monitoring, following or contacting that causes fear or distress.

🎭

Fake Profiles

Impersonation through fraudulent social media accounts or misuse of another person's identity.

🔒

Privacy Violations

Sharing private images, personal information or sensitive content without consent.

⚠

Online Blackmail

Threatening someone to obtain money, information or other benefits.

🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent messages or websites designed to steal passwords and financial information.

10 Essential Cyber Safety Tips

  1. Use strong and unique passwords.
  2. Enable two-factor authentication.
  3. Never share OTPs or banking passwords.
  4. Keep social media accounts private where appropriate.
  5. Avoid accepting unknown friend requests.
  6. Do not click suspicious links.
  7. Never share sensitive personal information publicly.
  8. Block and report abusive accounts.
  9. Preserve screenshots, messages and other evidence safely.
  10. Seek help from trusted people and official authorities.
