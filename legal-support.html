Important Legal Protections

Learn about important laws that support women's rights.

⚖

Domestic Violence Act, 2005

The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act provides civil remedies and protection for women facing domestic violence.

🏢

POSH Act, 2013

Provides a framework to prevent and address sexual harassment of women at the workplace.

👨‍👩‍👧

Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961

Prohibits the giving and taking of dowry as defined under the Act.

🛡

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

Contains criminal law provisions addressing offences including assault, stalking, sexual harassment and other crimes.

📜

Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987

Establishes the legal services framework for providing free legal services to eligible persons.

👩‍⚖

Right to Legal Aid

Eligible persons can seek free legal services through Legal Services Authorities. Women are among the eligible categories.

How to Seek Legal Assistance

  1. Reach a safe place: Prioritise personal safety.
  2. Contact support: Approach police, a recognised support service or a legal services authority as appropriate.
  3. Preserve evidence: Keep relevant messages, photographs, documents and other records safely.
  4. Seek legal advice: Consult a qualified lawyer or legal aid authority.
  5. Follow the procedure: Keep copies of complaints and reference numbers where available.
